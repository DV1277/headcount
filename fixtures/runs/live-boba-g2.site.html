Why it exists

Westwood goes quiet before the craving does

Research on nearby shops shows the closest boba to campus closes around 11pm on weeknights, and the Hill requires every guest to be signed in at an access desk between 9pm and 5am. That leaves a real gap after midnight.

Open when others are not

No Westwood boba shop reliably serves past midnight. A proposed 8pm–2am window targets the hours students told us they actually order.

Lobby handoff, not street drop

UCLA housing rules require guests to be signed in at access desks after 9pm, so we meet you in the lobby — never at your room door.

Group-friendly by design

Orders are built for the 2–4 person study or post-party group, so one delivery covers the whole room instead of four separate trips.

FAQ

Questions students actually asked

Is this service running right now?

No. This is a prototype landing page collecting interest before launch. Every price, hour and service area shown is a proposal, and no orders are being accepted yet.

How does the dorm lobby handoff work with UCLA rules?

UCLA on-campus housing requires guests to be signed in at an access desk between 9pm and 5am. The proposed model has the runner wait in the lobby area so you sign them in at the desk and collect your order there, rather than meeting at your room.

Why is there a four-cup pack?

Research shows late-night orders usually come from groups of two to four after studying or a night out. Batching those into one delivery keeps the per-drink price inside the $6.25–$8.15 range seen at nearby Westwood shops.
